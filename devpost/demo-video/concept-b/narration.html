Narration shot list

The rough cut (3:11, no sound; the app clips re-recorded in dark mode on Oct 25, the draft simulated and captioned, the statistics shot cut, crossfades between clips and a small label at the start of each section) is below. For each shot: what's on screen, the points to cover, and a box for your words. The words are yours: the hackathon's rules say the narration must be written by you. At a relaxed pace, people speak about 2 to 2.5 words a second, so each shot shows a rough word budget. What you type stays in this browser only; copy it somewhere safe when you're done.

Transcript 




The teleprompter plays the cut (muted) and shows your words large, greying in each word about when it's due, with a countdown before each new shot. "Record" records your voice from the Mac's microphone while the cut plays and saves an audio file that knows where in the cut it starts; a take can start from any shot. Send the files to Claude to lay over the cut (merge-voice.py next to this page does it with ffmpeg). To change the cuts, edit the times in rough-cut.sh and run it again.
