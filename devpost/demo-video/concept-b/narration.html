Narration shot list

The rough cut (2:37, no sound; the app clips re-recorded in dark mode on Oct 25, the draft simulated and captioned, the statistics shot cut) is below. For each shot: what's on screen, the points to cover, and a box for your words. The words are yours: the hackathon's rules say the narration must be written by you. At a relaxed pace, people speak about 2 to 2.5 words a second, so each shot shows a rough word budget. What you type stays in this browser only; copy it somewhere safe when you're done.

To change the cuts, edit the times in devpost/demo-video/concept-b/rough-cut.sh and run it again. To record your voice over it: QuickTime → New Audio Recording while the rough cut plays, or record straight into iMovie's voiceover tool.
