Demo video options

Three ways to tell the same loop in under three minutes. Each one has a storyboard and a short motion-graphics prototype, and they all use the same real recordings of the app. You pick, and you write the words: the captions and narration points here are placeholders.

The videos and pictures live in media/, which is gitignored. If they don't load, you're looking at a fresh clone: the recordings were made locally on Oct 21 and aren't in the repo.

What I'd pick: C, "Not a red X"

It opens on the problem. A viewer who stops after five seconds has still seen what acu-primer does: a wrong answer gets help aimed at that mistake, instead of a red X. Every claim after that is shown in a real recording, so nothing depends on a paid draft or a mock-up. The loop graphic introduces the author half in ten seconds, before the recordings show it.

I'd borrow one piece from B: its page-to-modules transition, laid over a screenshot of the real OpenIntro page, works well as the opener for the drafting beat (C's shot 6).

A book opens, its page turns into a question, and it answers a wrong pick in the margin. Then the real app does the same.

Strong at

Risk

A page becomes a lesson

Follow one textbook page until it's a lesson: modules, checks, a fallback for each wrong answer, a person's sign-off, then a student.

Strong at
Shows the AI part clearly, in the order the loop runs. Fits a "Build With AI" judge.
Risk
The student's moment arrives late (about 1:45). It needs one real paid draft on camera (about $1.30), or the transition looks like a mock-up.
Uses
Clips 01, 02, 04, 05; a new recording of Draft and of shaping the draft; the page-to-modules transition.

Not a red X

Open on the red X every quiz ends with, show acu-primer answering the mistake instead, then pull back to the loop that made that help.

Strong at
The problem and the answer in the first 15 seconds. Burned-in captions, so it works with the sound off.
Risk
The opener restages the app in motion graphics, so the real clips need to follow quickly to show it isn't a mock-up.

Real recordings

Recorded with Playwright on a local wrangler dev at 1280x720, as student "Amina" on the hand-written 5.1 lesson, and on the in-app Claude draft for the review. They're good enough for a rough cut. For the final cut, re-record them at 1920x1080 at the pace of your voice.

01 Wrong, then right (44 s). Name, intro with outcomes, a wrong pick, re-explanation and simpler question, retry, green with the explanation.

02 Fails the fallback (25 s, dark). Types the count, fails the simpler question, "go back through the material", Skip for now.

04 Author review (25 s). The gate, Author home, step-by-step review of the Claude draft, approvals, the resolved suggestion. Shows "Claude this month: $1.37 of $15".

05 Student log (11 s). The student list, then Amina's path with every answer, fallback, and the material suggestion.

06 Offline (23 s). Network off, reload, the course opens and module 2 plays and grades. The red pill is an overlay I added.

03 Light and dark (12 s). The switch cycles Auto, Light, Dark.

Tools for the final edit

  • Nothing new is required. The prototypes are plain HTML. They render to MP4 with Playwright (already on this Mac) and ffmpeg (in Homebrew). render.cjs in this folder does it; its header says how to run it.
  • An editor to cut it together: iMovie (already free on the Mac), DaVinci Resolve (free), or Shotcut.
  • Screen and voice: Cmd+Shift+5 or QuickTime is enough. Cap (free, open source) adds zooms and backgrounds.
  • Optional: Remotion, if you want the whole edit in code. It's free for individuals.
  • Music: none is the safe choice. The YouTube Audio Library is the easiest source if you want some.
