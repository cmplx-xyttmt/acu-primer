# Concept C: Not a red X

**Pitch:** open on the red X every quiz ends with, then show acu-primer answering the mistake instead, and pull back to the loop that made that help (drafted with Claude, checked by a person).

Told from the student's side first, because the fallback is the beat a stranger gets in five seconds. The author half arrives as the answer to "where did that help come from?"

Prototype: `index.html` (21 s, plays in a browser, click to pause). Render: `../media/renders/not-a-red-x.mp4`. It covers shots 1 to 3 and the loop graphic in shot 4.
