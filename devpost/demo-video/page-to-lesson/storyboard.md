# Concept B: A page becomes a lesson

**Pitch:** start with one page of a real textbook and follow it until it's a lesson a student plays: split into modules, given checks and fallbacks for each wrong answer, checked by a person, then played.

Told from the author's side, in the order the loop runs. It shows off the Claude part the most, so it fits a "Build With AI" judge. The cost is that the student's "oh" moment arrives later, around 1:20.

Prototype: `index.html` (20 s, plays in a browser, click to pause). Render: `../media/renders/page-to-lesson.mp4`. It's the key transition in shots 2-4: paragraphs lift off the page into module cards, one module opens into its check, wrong answers and fallbacks, and the review marks turn from "written by the model" to "reviewed by a person".
