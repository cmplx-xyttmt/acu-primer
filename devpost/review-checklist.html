Decisions to confirm

keep or change

Calls I made while you were away. Each is also recorded under Revisions in devpost/checklist.md. Mark keep or change.

Next slices: 4b, 5, then 7 (offline), skipping 3b and 63b needs your API key. Slice 6's core ("tell the tool what to change") needs it too, and its other parts overlap with 4b's suggestions, so I think 6 is best designed together with 3b.
A step with an open suggestion can't be approved, and adding a suggestion takes back the step's approvalApproving something you've asked to change would contradict itself. You can always withdraw a suggestion.
An approval stores a fingerprint (hash) of the step it approvedA revision that changes a step clears that step's approval and no other. Re-importing unchanged content keeps approvals and the sign-off.
The outcomes are review step 1They're part of what students see first, so they get approved too.
The student log lives at /author/courses/<course>/log?name=…&device=…A query string instead of the spec's path key, because a typed name can contain any character.
Events are sent ~2 seconds after activity, plus on start, when back online, and every 30 secondsThe spec said start, online and every 30 s; the extra "soon after activity" makes the log near-live for you.
Offline: only woff2 font files are stored, and course data waits 5 s for the network before using the saved copyEvery browser with service workers reads woff2, so the other formats would be dead weight. 5 s keeps a slow connection from hiding fresh content.
Drafting stays on Opus 5.5 at high effort for now ($1.28 a draft)Cheaper options: effort medium (the model's default; my guess $0.50–0.70), no alternatives in the first draft (about a quarter less output), or Sonnet 5 (DRAFT_MODEL, half price). Your call: mark "change" and say which.
A $15 monthly limit on Claude calls, logged per callLeaves $5 of your $20 as a cushion. Change MONTHLY_BUDGET_USD in wrangler.jsonc.
Drafting rules made subject-neutral, plus a per-course brief and an optional catalogueFrom the OSTEP list: "define every term and symbol"; "mostly explain why, compare, predict or apply; recall at most a third"; vary the right answer's position. Your notes said rule changes are your call, so: keep or reverse?
Claude writes a flat "wire" format that the Worker convertsThe API refused our lesson schema as too big a grammar. Nothing you see changed; it's the only way structured outputs accept it.
Shaping lives on the review screen, and any edit needs the step approved againSwaps, switches and revisions are next to the parts they change; a sign-off is voided by any edit.
In-app drafts live only in the database, not in content/ filesA draft made on the live site lives in the live database; my local test draft is only local. An "export to a file" button could come later if you want drafts in git.
