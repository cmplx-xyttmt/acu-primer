newno Claude calls

Approving writes to your local database; you can take an approval back on the step. Don't press Apply with Claude here unless you mean to (about $0.05–0.30).

  1. AUTHORAuthor home → Review on "5.1 Point estimates and sampling variability" (the unreviewed one)
    A violet edge round the window and a violet bar: "Review mode · You're playing this lesson as a student would." A Play / Every branch switch under the title, Play selected.
  2. AUTHORGo to step 2 and read the coach line and the list on the right
    "Answer as a student would. Start with the right answer…" and "Paths seen 0 / 4": the right answer, each anticipated wrong answer, any other answer.
  3. AUTHORPick a wrong answer and Check
    It shakes and turns red; the hint appears as the student sees it, with a violet Author's view box above it (the misconception, catalogue or the model's guess, the "shown to students" switches). That path ticks.
  4. AUTHORAnswer again, try the other paths; for "Any other answer" press show it
    Each ticks in turn; the coach line counts down, then says "That's every path."
  5. AUTHOROn another module, press Approve module before trying everything
    It asks once: "You haven't tried n paths yet: … Approve anyway?" with Keep playing.
  6. AUTHORHover the reading or a hint and click the small violet button in its margin
    A note box for that part. A saved note works as before: the step can't be approved while it's open.
  7. AUTHORSwitch to Every branch, then back to Play
    The old all-branches page, without the frame; back in Play your ticks are still there.
  8. AUTHORA number check (step 4)
    The list names the ranges to type ("Types 0 to 0.0002") and the right answer with its tolerance.

Draft in the app (slice 3b)

costs money: one at most

These cost money: a ten-page draft took 10½ minutes and $1.28, a revision about $0.09. The app stops at $15 a month and shows the spend on the author home. One draft at most, ideally a short reading.

  1. AUTHORMake sure the subject has a learner brief (section 02, s8)
    Every draft in the subject follows it.
  2. AUTHOROn a chapter, Draft a lesson
    The spend so far, the brief that applies, fields for the PDF (a styled Choose PDF button), book title, pages, section, and Draft.
  3. AUTHORPick a short PDF (3–5 pages), fill in the fields, press Draft
    "Cutting…", "Sending…", then "Claude is thinking about the pages (n s)" for a few minutes, then a growing character count. Keep the tab open.
  4. AUTHORWhen it says Drafted
    The time and cost, and Review it step by step; the lesson is listed with a Draft badge. Does it follow the brief?

Shape a draft (slice 6)

one paid revise at most
  1. AUTHORReviewing a draft, open 1 alternative check → Use this check instead
    The check is replaced; the old one becomes the alternative (swap back the same way). The step needs approving again.
  2. AUTHOROn a wrong answer's re-explanation, untick shown to students
    It dims and says "(switched off)"; a student getting that wrong answer sees only the simpler question.
  3. AUTHORLeave a suggestion, then Apply with Claude (about $0.05–0.30)
    A note like "Claude changed: reading ($0.092)"; the suggestion is Resolved; other steps keep their approvals.
  4. AUTHORModule 2 of the API draft ("Point estimates and sampling variability", the third lesson in the sampling chapter)
    My test revision added the 5,000-vs-500 students sentence and changed nothing else.
