Logo options for acu-primer

Each mark is drawn in the new look you picked: warm paper and teal, with the sun yellow from Bright steps. Each one is shown on paper and at night, next to the name, and at favicon size (the hardest test: it has to read at 16 pixels). All are inspired by the Young Lady's Illustrated Primer in The Diamond Age, the book that teaches its reader and changes its story to fit her. None copies anything from the novel's covers.

To choose: tell Claude a number (or two to combine, e.g. "1's book with 5's path"), whether you want the name next to it set in a serif as shown, and anything to change (colour, shape). A plain wordmark, option 8, is always possible too.
